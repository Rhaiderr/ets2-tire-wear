ETS2 & ATS Tire Wear - alpha
============================

EN: Shows your truck and trailer tires worn according to the real in-game
wear, in Euro Truck Simulator 2 and American Truck Simulator. Traffic keeps
its tires. Tested only on ETS2 1.61 and ATS 1.61, DirectX 11.
Tire mods are not supported yet: a modded tire keeps its original look.

PT: Mostra os pneus do seu caminhao e das suas carretas gastos de acordo com
o desgaste real do jogo, no Euro Truck Simulator 2 e no American Truck
Simulator. O trafego fica como esta. Testado so no ETS2 1.61 e no ATS 1.61,
DirectX 11.
Pneus de mods ainda nao sao compativeis: um pneu de mod fica com a aparencia
original.

INSTALL / INSTALAR (game closed / jogo fechado)
Copy ets2_tire_wear.dll to / Copie ets2_tire_wear.dll para:
  <game folder / pasta do jogo>\bin\win_x64\plugins\
  (create "plugins" if needed / crie a pasta "plugins" se nao existir)
  ETS2 (Steam): C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Euro Truck Simulator 2\bin\win_x64\plugins\
  ATS  (Steam): C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\American Truck Simulator\bin\win_x64\plugins\
  The same DLL works in both games / A mesma DLL serve nos dois jogos.
Start the game and accept the "advanced SDK features" warning.
Abra o jogo e confirme o aviso "advanced SDK features".

Settings and logs / Configuracao e logs (created on first run / criada na primeira execucao):
  Documents\Euro Truck Simulator 2\TireWear\
  Documents\American Truck Simulator\TireWear\

UNINSTALL / DESINSTALAR
Delete / Apague plugins\ets2_tire_wear.dll and / e Documents\<game / jogo>\TireWear.
