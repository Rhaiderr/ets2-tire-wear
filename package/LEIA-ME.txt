ETS2 & ATS Tire Wear - beta
===========================

EN: Shows your truck and trailer tires worn according to the real in-game
wear, in Euro Truck Simulator 2 and American Truck Simulator. Traffic keeps
its tires. Worn tires can BLOW OUT (from 60% wear): a notice, sounds, a
shredded tire, the truck pulls and is held to 60 km/h until you replace the
tires. On a force feedback wheel the pull is a force on the wheel (tested on
a Logitech G923). Tested on ETS2 1.61 and ATS 1.61, DirectX 11, single player.
Tire mods are not supported yet: a modded tire keeps its original look.

PT: Mostra os pneus do seu caminhao e das suas carretas gastos de acordo com
o desgaste real do jogo, no Euro Truck Simulator 2 e no American Truck
Simulator. O trafego fica como esta. Pneus gastos podem ESTOURAR (a partir de
60% de desgaste): aviso, som, pneu rasgado, o caminhao puxa e fica limitado a
60 km/h ate voce trocar os pneus. Num volante com force feedback a puxada e
uma forca no volante (testado num Logitech G923). Testado no ETS2 1.61 e no
ATS 1.61, DirectX 11, single player.
Pneus de mods ainda nao sao compativeis: um pneu de mod fica com a aparencia
original.

INSTALL / INSTALAR (game closed / jogo fechado)
Copy BOTH files / Copie OS DOIS arquivos:
  ets2_tire_wear.dll
  ets2_tire_wear.ini
to / para:
  <game folder / pasta do jogo>\bin\win_x64\plugins\
  (create "plugins" if needed / crie a pasta "plugins" se nao existir)
  ETS2 (Steam): C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Euro Truck Simulator 2\bin\win_x64\plugins\
  ATS  (Steam): C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\American Truck Simulator\bin\win_x64\plugins\
  The same files work in both games / Os mesmos arquivos servem nos dois jogos.
Start the game and accept the "advanced SDK features" warning.
Abra o jogo e confirme o aviso "advanced SDK features".

SETTINGS / CONFIGURACAO
plugins\ets2_tire_wear.ini (edit with the game closed / edite com o jogo fechado).
Turn blowouts off / Desligar os estouros: [blowout] enabled = 0
Logs: Documents\<game / jogo>\TireWear\

UNINSTALL / DESINSTALAR
Delete / Apague plugins\ets2_tire_wear.dll, plugins\ets2_tire_wear.ini and / e
Documents\<game / jogo>\TireWear.
