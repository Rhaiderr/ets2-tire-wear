# 15-second blowout GIF / video script

Goal: show the blowout in the first 3 seconds of the README. Record in-game, 1080p, then export a GIF of at most ~8 MB (under 1280 px wide) and keep the MP4 for the forum/Reddit.

Setup: use `testes\3-estouro-caminhao.ini` (blows the truck's front left tire 20 s after you drive off). Worn tires help: or use `2-ver-desgaste.ini` first for the shot at 0:00.

| Time | Shot | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:03 | Exterior shot of a rear wheel with the worn tread (`2-ver-desgaste.ini`, 86%) | Shows the visual wear before anything else |
| 0:03-0:06 | Cab view, driving on a highway; the wheel moves slightly | Real driving, not a menu |
| 0:06-0:09 | The blowout: on-screen notice at the top, truck jolts to the side | Include the notice; it is the clearest proof |
| 0:09-0:13 | Exterior shot of the blown tire (shredded) while the truck keeps pulling | If a wheel cam is available, show the hands on the wheel |
| 0:13-0:15 | Text card: "ETS2 + ATS tire wear & blowouts: github.com/Rhaiderr/ets2-tire-wear" | One line, no music needed |

Rules: only the plugin's own behavior, nothing edited in post. Say "Logitech G923" if the wheel is shown, since it is the only wheel tested. Do not include your Windows user name in any overlay.
Output names: `docs/images/blowout.gif` (README) and `blowout.mp4` (posts).
